## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional yet deeply human EdTech environment engineered for K-12 and higher education platforms. The aesthetic bridges the gap between enterprise-grade reliability and student-centered warmth, rejecting cold, bureaucratic administration tables in favor of clear, empowering, and transparent interfaces.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Steadfast:** Conveys deep organizational security, audit readiness, and institutional integrity without feeling rigid or dated.
- **Optimistic & Growth-Oriented:** Emphasizes student achievement, milestones, and constructive feedback loops using clean color signaling.
- **Frictionless & Accessible:** Minimizes cognitive fatigue for educators, registrars, parents, and students through generous spatial rhythm, high legibility, and predictable interaction models.

### Design Movement: Modern Institutional Glass & Card Architecture
The visual framework merges crisp corporate SaaS precision with tactile modern cards, subtle background translucency (frosted navigation rails, contextual status banners), refined micro-borders, and generous corner smoothing. The resulting interface feels polished, modern, and enduring across complex academic workflows from large-scale admissions pipelines to daily grading and attendance logs.

## Colors

The color system delivers high organizational authority through deep navy foundations, paired with vitalizing greens and achievement-oriented ambers.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#0F2540`):** Institutional Navy. Used for core structural boundaries, top-level headings, active navigation containers, and high-priority commands. For lighter states or focused hover borders, utilize secondary tint `#1A365D`.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Growth Emerald. Signifies academic progress, enrollment completion, verified credentials, and positive system notifications. Deep state variant: `#059669`.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Milestone Amber. Highlights tuition balances pending, alert statuses, honors, and critical workflow warnings. Light accent tint: `#FBBF24`.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Slate. Forms secondary typography, passive borders, and neutral tab structures.

### Surface Tiers & Typography Neutrals
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) establishes a glare-free, comforting background across wide desktop canvases.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card/Container):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with fine micro-borders (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Nested Panels / Table Headers):** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100).
- **Text Primary:** `#0F172A` (Slate 900) ensures crisp AA/AAA contrast ratios against light surfaces.
- **Text Secondary:** `#334155` (Slate 700) for subheads and critical metadata.
- **Text Muted:** `#64748B` (Slate 500) for timestamps, non-interactive labels, and helper text.

## Typography

The typographic system combines the geometric friendliness and modern authority of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headers with the systematic optical clarity of **Inter** for dense dashboards, rosters, gradebooks, and inputs.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Principles
- **Display & Headings:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers welcoming curves and generous counterforms, preventing administrative dashboards from feeling punishing or impersonal.
- **Body & Data:** Inter provides balanced vertical alignment and tall x-height, guaranteeing optimal rendering across dense numerical tables, transcript layouts, and multi-line institutional policy documents.
- **Kerning & Letter Spacing:** Headings maintain a slightly tightened tracking (`-0.015em`) at display sizes for editorial weight, while `label-sm` utilizes expanded tracking (`+0.04em`) to ensure status tags and chips remain scannable from distance.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts rely on an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around an 8pt base grid for clean layout alignment and predictable spatial rhythms.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rules
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer canvas padding (`margin-lg`), `1.5rem` gutters. Persistent left institutional navigation rail (260px fixed width).
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px to 1279px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` margin and `1rem` gutters. Navigation collapses to an icon-rail (72px) or a sliding institutional drawer.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Stacks grade summaries, enrollment steps, and data cards vertically into single full-width components.

### Density Handling
Complex SIS (Student Information System) pages such as course registration schedules and batch grading grids switch dynamically from default card spacing (`space-lg`) to compact spatial mode (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to increase data density without horizontal scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system expresses hierarchy through precise tonal stacking, translucent glass panels, and diffuse, slate-tinted ambient drop shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphic projections.

### Tiers of Depth
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. Base background where all components reside.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 37, 64, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 37, 64, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Flyouts):** Used for hovered cards, active task rows, and dropdown menus: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 37, 64, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 37, 64, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Enrollment Drawers & Overlays):** Deep ambient spread: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 37, 64, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 37, 64, 0.06)`. Accompanied by a 24% opacity Slate-900 frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`).

### Glassmorphism & Translucency
Top headers, quick-action navigation bars, and enrollment sticky ribbons utilize an institutional frosted aesthetic: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` background with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle bottom border (`1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`). This anchors the user in long documents without obscuring context.

## Shapes

The design system uses a Level 2 (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline) shape language with intentional progression up to `12px` and `16px` for structural cards, balancing friendly modern contours with institutional dignity.

### Scale of Application
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Menu Dropdowns:** `0.5rem` (8px). Ensures a balanced, clickable contour that aligns cleanly alongside form labels and grid lines.
- **Cards, Panels, and Modules (`rounded-lg`):** `0.75rem` to `1rem` (12px to 16px). Softens large content sections, creating an inviting, student-friendly frame.
- **Modals, Dialogs, and Hero Modules (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px). Creates an approachable, contemporary presence for pivotal milestones.
- **Status Badges, Indicator Dots, and Avatar Frames:** Full pill (`9999px`), ensuring distinction from interactive rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F2540`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`. On hover: `#1A365D` with subtle `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 37, 64, 0.12)` lift. Active: `#0F2540`.
- **Secondary (Growth / Success):** Background `#10B981`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#059669`. Used for pivotal progress actions ("Submit Application", "Finalize Grades").
- **Outline / Neutral:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#334155`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#94A3B8`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#0F2540`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Shape & Typography:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `4px 10px`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Verified / Enrolled:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- **Attention / Pending Action:** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.
- **Institutional / Inactive:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `12px` or `16px` border-radius, `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border, and Level 1 elevation.
- Internal padding matches `space-lg` (`24px`). When interactive (e.g., student course selection card), hover state transitions border color to `#1A365D` and applies Level 2 elevation.

### Input Fields
- Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, corner radius `8px`, typography `body-md`.
- **Focus State:** Border changes to `#0F2540` accompanied by a distinct 3px outer ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 37, 64, 0.12)`.
- **Error State:** Border `#EF4444` with a red focus ring (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`) and explicit inline error messaging in `body-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Sized `18px × 18px` with `4px` radius for checkboxes and circular radius for radios.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #94A3B8`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#0F2540` with white vector checkmark/dot. Smooth 150ms ease-in-out transition.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Progress Milestone Tracker:** Step-by-step horizontal train representing admissions or degree audit completion. Completed milestones display emerald check rings with connector lines in `#10B981`; active milestones show a navy badge with pulsating outer rings.
- **Academic Metric Tile:** Compact dashboard widget containing a prominent score/GPA figure in `headline-lg`, accompanied by contextual micro-trend indicators (e.g., "+0.2 this semester" pill in growth green).
